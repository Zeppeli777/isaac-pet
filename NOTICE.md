The additional character atlases (`Resources/Agents/<role>-*.webp` for Cain, Judas, Blue Baby, Eve, Samson, Azazel, Lazarus, Eden, The Lost, Lilith, Keeper, Apollyon, The Forgotten, Bethany, and Jacob & Esau) are deterministic derivations of the original player sprite sheets shipped with the game itself (`Assets/Source/game-sheets/`): twelve sheets come from the local Afterbirth+ installation owned by the project author, and the three Repentance-only sheets (Bethany, Jacob, Esau) were retrieved from the fan-maintained mirror [zth12138/isaac-dress-up](https://github.com/zth12138/isaac-dress-up). The scripts only relocate heads, overlay default costume pieces, and remap body palettes onto the approved Isaac desktop-pet poses; no pixels are generated or redrawn.
